An independent French mobile app studio. We design, build and launch iPhone and Android apps, and open-source the native pieces we make along the way.

## Apps

| App | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Splitify](https://splitify.cc) | Our own app. Split expenses with friends: no account, no ads, no limits. On the App Store and Google Play. |
| [EquiPlanner](https://equiplanner.fr) | Client work. An iPhone and Android app that becomes the life logbook of your horses. |

## Open source

What we built for Splitify and extracted: native UI libraries that use the platform's own components instead of redrawing them in JS, and the release pipeline the app ships with.

| Library | What it does |
|---|---|
| [react-native-alert](https://github.com/27AppStudio/react-native-alert) | Native alerts and text prompts in your app's colors |
| [react-native-bottom-toolbar](https://github.com/27AppStudio/react-native-bottom-toolbar) | The platform's own bottom toolbar: UIKit on iOS, Material 3 on Android |
| [react-native-emoji-picker](https://github.com/27AppStudio/react-native-emoji-picker) | A native emoji picker: iOS popover, Material 3 bottom sheet |
| [rn-ship](https://github.com/27AppStudio/rn-ship) | EAS-style release pipeline for bare React Native apps |
